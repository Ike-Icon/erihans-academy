Program 02

Money management

Most budgets fail within a month because they were never built around how you actually live. This program builds one that survives contact with real life, and a plan to clear debt without the guilt spiral.

You'll leave with a working budget built from your own numbers, not a generic template, plus a clear order for paying down whatever debt you're carrying, and a savings target sized to your actual monthly costs rather than a round number someone else picked.

This is the program most people take first, because it fixes the leak before you try to fill the bucket. Everything else, investing, building a business, saving for something big, gets easier once this part is under control.

  • Building a budget around your real income and spending, not an idealized version of it
  • Paying down debt in the order that costs you the least over time
  • Sizing an emergency fund to your actual expenses, not a generic "three months" rule
  • Separating needs, wants and "future you" money so nothing quietly disappears
  • What to do the week money runs short, before it becomes a crisis
