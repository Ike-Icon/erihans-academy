Program 05

Wealth creation

A single income, however well managed, has a ceiling. This program is about raising that ceiling: negotiating what you're already worth, pricing side income properly, and understanding how what you earn can compound instead of just sitting still.

Wealth creation isn't one big move, it's several smaller ones stacked over time: negotiating a raise instead of assuming the first offer is final, pricing freelance or side work so it's actually worth your time, and directing what you earn so it compounds rather than evaporates.

This program picks up where money management leaves off. Once your budget and debt are under control, this is where the growth actually happens.

  • Negotiating salary and rates with actual numbers behind you
  • Pricing freelance or side income so it's worth the hours you put in
  • Building a second income stream without burning out your first job
  • The maths behind compounding, and why starting small now beats waiting for "enough"
  • Turning a raise or windfall into lasting wealth instead of lifestyle creep
