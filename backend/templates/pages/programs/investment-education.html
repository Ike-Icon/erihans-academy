Program 03

Investment education

Investing without understanding what you own is just a different kind of gambling. This program teaches you to evaluate an investment on its own terms, so you can say why you own something, not just that someone told you to buy it.

We start with your timeline and how much risk you can actually sleep through, not the other way around. From there you'll learn how to compare investment options on cost, risk and liquidity, so you're choosing deliberately instead of copying whatever's trending.

This isn't stock-picking hype. It's the groundwork: what compounding actually looks like over years, what fees quietly cost you, and how to avoid the schemes designed to separate you from your money.

  • Matching an investment's timeline and risk level to your own situation
  • How fees and charges erode returns over time, and what's reasonable to pay
  • The difference between investing, speculating and gambling, and why it matters
  • Spotting an investment scheme before it costs you
  • Building a plan you can stick with when the market gets uncomfortable
