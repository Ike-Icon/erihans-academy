Program 01

Financial education

The foundation everything else is built on. Before you can budget, invest or grow a business well, you need to understand how money actually moves, what a lender is really offering you, and why your payslip looks the way it does.

Most people manage money the way they learned it at home, by trial and error. This program replaces the guesswork with a working understanding of interest, inflation, credit and tax, so every financial decision after this one is made with your eyes open.

Sessions are hands-on. You'll work with your own bank statements, your own payslip, and real loan offers, not hypothetical examples, so what you learn applies immediately to your situation.

  • How compound interest works for you when you save, and against you when you borrow
  • Reading a bank statement, loan agreement and payslip without needing anyone's help
  • What inflation actually does to money sitting idle, and what to do about it
  • The basics of personal tax, and what's actually deducted from your income and why
  • Spotting predatory credit terms before you sign anything
