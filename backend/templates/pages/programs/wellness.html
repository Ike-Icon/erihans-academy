Program 06

Wellness

Financial progress that costs you your health isn't progress. This program runs alongside every other course, so the stress of fixing your money doesn't quietly wear you down while you do it.

Money stress shows up in places people don't expect: disrupted sleep, shorter tempers, avoiding a partner's calls, putting off a doctor's visit. This program treats that as directly relevant to your financial goals, because it is.

Sessions are practical rather than abstract: naming what financial stress is actually doing to your body, setting boundaries around money conversations that used to spiral, and building rest into a plan that would otherwise burn you out.

  • Recognizing the physical and emotional signs of money-related stress
  • Setting boundaries in money conversations with family, partners and friends
  • Building rest and recovery into a financial plan instead of treating it as optional
  • Simple grounding techniques for high-stress money moments
  • Keeping pace with a financial plan without burning out halfway through
