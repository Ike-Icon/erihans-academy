Program 04

Entrepreneurship

Most small businesses don't fail because the idea was bad. They fail because the basics, pricing, cash flow, keeping business and personal money separate, never got sorted out. This program sorts them out first.

You'll work through the unglamorous parts that actually determine whether a business survives: pricing your product or service so it covers real costs, keeping cash flow visible so you're never surprised, and separating your business finances from your personal ones from day one.

If you already have a business running, this program is just as useful, most of it applies to fixing what's currently held together by guesswork.

  • Pricing that covers your real costs and still leaves room for profit
  • Keeping cash flow visible so a slow month doesn't become a crisis
  • Separating business money from personal money, and why it matters more than it seems
  • The basic paperwork and registration steps most new business owners skip
  • When to reinvest in the business and when to pay yourself first
