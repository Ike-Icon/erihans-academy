Five programs, one plan

Each one stands on its own. Together, they take you from "I don't know where my money goes" to running a household budget, an investment portfolio and maybe a business, without burning out.

  1. 01

    Financial education

    Understand how money actually works: interest, inflation, credit, tax. You leave able to read a bank statement, a loan offer and a payslip without help.

  2. 02

    Wealth creation

    Build more than one stream of income. We cover side income, salary negotiation, freelance pricing and the maths behind compounding what you earn.

  3. 03

    Money management

    A budget you'll still be using in a year, a plan to pay down debt in order of cost, and an emergency fund sized to your real expenses.

  4. 04

    Investment & entrepreneurship

    Pick investments that match your timeline and risk appetite, and learn what it takes to start and run a small business without guessing your way through it.

  5. 05

    Wellness

    Money stress affects sleep, focus and relationships. Weekly sessions on stress management, boundaries and rest, so financial progress doesn't cost you your health.
